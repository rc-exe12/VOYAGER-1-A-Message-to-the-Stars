# VOYAGER 1 --- A Message to the Stars

> **A digital tribute to humanity's farthest-travelling spacecraft.**\
> A cinematic journey through the Voyager 1 mission, its discoveries,
> the Golden Record, and a symbolic message from Earth.

## 🌌 About the Project

**VOYAGER 1 --- A Message to the Stars** is an interactive, single-page
website created to celebrate NASA's Voyager 1 and its extraordinary
journey beyond the outer planets and into interstellar space.

The experience combines mission information, visual storytelling, and an
interactive Morse Code sequence to express a simple message of
appreciation to a spacecraft that has carried a piece of Earth into the
unknown.

## ✨ Features

-   **Cinematic space experience** --- dark, deep-space styling,
    animated starfield, and spacecraft visuals.
-   **Voyager 1 tracker** --- displays distance and mission-related
    estimates with context about how the values are calculated.
-   **Mission timeline** --- highlights important milestones in Voyager
    1's journey.
-   **The Golden Record** --- introduces the gold-plated record and its
    purpose as a representation of life and culture on Earth.
-   **Discoveries and FAQs** --- presents mission information in an
    accessible format.
-   **Pale Blue Dot section** --- explains the famous image and its
    significance, with a link to NASA's original image.
-   **The Last Transmission** --- an animated International Morse Code
    sequence that symbolically sends a thank-you message from Earth
    towards Voyager 1.
-   **Interactive controls** --- start, pause, resume, replay, and stop
    the animation; adjust playback speed and sound.
-   **Responsive and accessible details** --- viewport-aware layout,
    keyboard focus styles, and reduced-motion support.

## 💌 The Message

The symbolic Morse Code transmission carries this message:

> THANK YOU VOYAGER 1. YOU HAVE DONE GREAT. BEST OF LUCK.

It is a visual tribute only. **The website does not transmit a radio
signal or send a message to the actual Voyager 1 spacecraft.**

## 📡 About the Tracker

The displayed tracker values are **calculated estimates**, not a live
connection to NASA telemetry or a spacecraft GPS feed. The page
describes its estimate baseline and assumptions. Because Voyager 1's
motion and Earth's orbit affect the changing distance, the displayed
rate should be understood as an approximation.

For authoritative mission information and ephemeris data, consult the
official NASA/JPL resources linked below.
